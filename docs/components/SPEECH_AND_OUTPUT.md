# Speech and Output

The output planner creates SpeechActs for progress, clarification, result, failure, uncertainty, divergence, or correction. Consequential acts use controlled templates; nonconsequential explanation may use model phrasing after policy validation. Approved acts enter one ordered session queue and produce start/finish events through a text/TTS `OutputPort`.

Before output dispatch, cancellation transitions approved speech to `CANCELLED` and prevents emission; after `EmitOutput` has been dispatched (`QUEUED` or `EMITTING`), `CancelSpeech` is cooperative and asks the adapter to stop playback while the reducer waits for adapter terminal facts to record whether audio was heard. It does not cancel operations. Already played audio remains `EMITTED`. Progressive Truth advances only when corresponding evidence exists, and skips stages a provider cannot prove. Adapter failure emits `SpeechEmissionFailed` (carrying `speech_id`, `error_code`, `heard`, `retryable`) and retains approved text for UI without automatic retry.

Approvals from TRUTHLOCK carry sequence freshness pin `through_sequence` and exact claim version map `claim_versions` (matching `ClaimRecord.updated_by_event_id`). Stale approvals retry `ValidateSpeech` without advancing to `APPROVED`. If a supporting claim changes while speech is `QUEUED` or `EMITTING`, the reducer records `cancellation_pending=True` and `correction_pending=True` and emits `CancelSpeech`; upon adapter finish (`SpeechEmissionFinished`) or failure (`SpeechEmissionFailed`), if `heard=True`, the final state becomes `CORRECTION_REQUIRED` and the reducer emits `RequestSpeechCorrection`. If `heard=False`, the speech transitions to `CANCELLED` without requesting correction. Pre-queue invalidation or cancellation moves `APPROVED` speech to `CANCELLED`. Tests: T-SPK-01.
