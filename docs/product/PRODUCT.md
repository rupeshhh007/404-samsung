# Product Specification

INTERLOCK lets an agent remain responsive without confusing current intent, obsolete execution, external reality, and what it may honestly say. Its users are people interacting with real-time assistants and teams building assistants that call consequential tools.

Interruptibility is a consistency problem: stopping speech does not cancel a booking; cancelling a local coroutine does not undo a remote commit; a late result can still describe reality; and a fluent model can overstate certainty. INTERLOCK therefore **anticipates** safe work with BranchCache, **adapts** execution at SAFEPOINT boundaries, and **verifies** claims through ClaimGraph and TRUTHLOCK.

The primary experience is quick provisional feedback, explicit clarification for ambiguous consequential changes, visible progress, surfaced divergence, and a final confirmation only after authoritative evidence. Success means the deterministic demo reproduces the correction race, retains its late effect, repairs it when allowed, and never announces an unsupported booking.

Expected capabilities include semantic control, provisional/committed intent, selective dependency invalidation, safe speculation, operation/effect separation, idempotency, reconciliation, immutable provenance, claim state, controlled speech, deterministic faults, trace exploration, and metrics.

Non-goals are a generic chatbot, scheduler, Samsung booking product, multi-agent swarm, identity platform, payment system, custom ASR/TTS/model training, microservices, or universal hallucination elimination. The Samsung Device/Service Copilot is a simulated demonstration domain; the runtime remains tool-manifest-driven and domain-generic.
